# Location Verification

This directory contains research and documentation relating to the verification of individual locations in the Ocarina of Time remake.

The purpose of location verification is to establish the reliability of location information recorded in the location catalog, document supporting evidence, identify discrepancies, and track the status of unresolved findings.

## Scope

Verification research may include:

- Confirming the existence of individual locations.
- Confirming location names and identities.
- Confirming area associations.
- Confirming check types.
- Confirming original rewards.
- Confirming relevant conditions or behavior.
- Documenting discrepancies between sources.
- Recording testing methods and results.
- Identifying unverified or disputed information.

## Verification Status

The following statuses may be used when documenting location information:

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Verified** | Confirmed through direct observation, reproducible testing, or sufficiently reliable supporting evidence. |
| **Partially Verified** | Some aspects of the information have been confirmed, but other aspects remain uncertain or unverified. |
| **Unverified** | Information has been recorded but has not yet been sufficiently confirmed. |
| **Disputed** | Conflicting evidence, observations, or sources exist and the discrepancy has not been resolved. |

These statuses are intended to communicate the reliability of findings and should not be treated as a measure of research priority.
