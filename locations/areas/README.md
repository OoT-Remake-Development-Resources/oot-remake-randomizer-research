# Location Areas

This directory contains research and documentation relating to the organization of item-bearing locations by their associated game areas.

The purpose of this directory is to establish a consistent understanding of how locations are grouped spatially and contextually throughout the Ocarina of Time remake.

## Scope

Area research may include:

- Area names and naming conventions.
- Area boundaries and distinctions.
- Relationships between areas.
- Subareas and nested area organization.
- Locations associated with each area.
- Alternate names and abbreviations.
- Discrepancies in area identification.

## Area Organization

Areas may be organized hierarchically where appropriate.

For example, a broader region may contain multiple subareas, which may in turn contain individual checks.

The hierarchy should reflect meaningful distinctions in the game rather than being based exclusively on naming conventions.

## Area Identification

Area assignments should be consistent and supported by observations or references when possible.

Where the boundaries or classification of an area are ambiguous, document the ambiguity rather than assuming a definitive classification.

## Relationship to Locations

Area documentation organizes and contextualizes individual locations but should not replace the master location catalog.

Individual checks should ultimately reference their associated area or areas using consistent identifiers or conventions.

## Future Development

Area organization may eventually support:

- Location catalog organization.
- Tracker grouping and navigation.
- Map integrations.
- Logic region organization.
- Location filtering and search.
- Structured area data.

The specific data format and hierarchy should remain flexible until sufficient research has established the requirements.
