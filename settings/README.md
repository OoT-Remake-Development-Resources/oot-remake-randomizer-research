# Randomizer Settings

This directory contains research and documentation relating to configurable settings and options for the Ocarina of Time remake randomizer.

Settings research aims to identify, document, and understand the options that may influence randomizer generation, gameplay, logic, and player experience.

## Scope

Settings research may include:

- Item pool configuration.
- Location shuffle options.
- Entrance shuffle options.
- Dungeon configuration.
- Logic difficulty.
- Optional tricks.
- Starting inventory.
- Goal configuration.
- Hint configuration.
- Progression options.
- Accessibility options.
- Multiworld configuration, where applicable.

These examples are provisional and do not represent a finalized settings list.

## Settings Documentation

Where possible, settings should be documented with:

- Setting name.
- Description.
- Available values or options.
- Default value, when known.
- Relevant behavior.
- Dependencies or interactions with other settings.
- Related logic or generation behavior.
- Verification status.
- References.

## Setting Interactions

Some settings may affect or depend on other settings.

Research should document known interactions, conflicts, dependencies, and limitations rather than treating every setting as independent.

## Relationship to Other Domains

- **Logic:** Settings may influence the requirements or rules used by the randomizer.
- **Items:** Settings may alter item pools, item availability, or starting inventory.
- **Locations:** Settings may influence location availability or shuffle behavior.
- **Testing:** Setting combinations may require validation.
- **Trackers:** Settings may influence tracker features or displayed information.

## Research Guidelines

- Document observable behavior.
- Distinguish confirmed behavior from assumptions.
- Record setting dependencies.
- Identify limitations and unresolved questions.
- Avoid prematurely defining a final configuration interface.

## Future Development

Settings research may eventually support a formal configuration system used by randomizer generation, logic validation, and tracker applications.

The configuration structure should remain flexible until sufficient research has established the requirements.
